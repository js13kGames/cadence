The game was designed by Nastka, age 12.

Fire a unicorn out of a cannon and surf the rainbow hills for as long as you can.
A storm is closing in behind you the whole time, and the only way to stay ahead
of it is to keep your speed up.

Line up the cannon, stop the ignition meter in the green for a perfect launch,
then ride the hills. Press the right arrow once per beat, exactly as the ring
closes around the unicorn: every clean step adds speed and builds your combo,
while a mistimed one costs you both. Hooves need ground, so the rhythm only
works while you are touching a hill. Dive to land on downslopes and keep rolling.

Galloping spends breath. Stars refill it, clean landings give a little back, and
if you run dry you have three seconds to reach a star before the run ends. Grab
a pair of wings and the beat moves to the up arrow, where flapping in rhythm
costs nothing and carries you forward.

Watch for rainbow strips that boost you, cotton candy that drags you down, comets,
portals, and thunderclouds that leave you too dizzy to keep time.

| Control | Action |
| --- | --- |
| **→** | Gallop. One press per beat, as the ring closes |
| **Space** / **W** / **↑** | Jump. With wings, tap on the beat to fly, hold to glide |
| **S** / **↓** | Dive. Land on a downslope to keep your speed |
| **M** | Mute |

On a touch screen: tap anywhere to gallop, drag downward to dive, and use the
on-screen button to jump or flap.
